🔬 Model Explainability & Domain Confusion Analysis

🏜️ Barren vs. Built-Up Confusion Diagnostic

Spectral Overlap: Both Barren soil and Built-up concrete exhibit elevated SWIR reflectance (B11/B12) and low NDVI.

Mitigation: BSI (Bare Soil Index) and NBR (Normalized Burn Ratio) are combined with a 2.5x class weighting penalty to isolate uncultivated rocky soils from urban structures.

🌾 Agriculture vs. Vegetation Diagnostic

Spectral Overlap: Natural trees and crops both exhibit high Near-Infrared (B8) reflectance. Harvesting cycles create temporary soil exposure.

Mitigation: Soil-Adjusted Vegetation Index (SAVI) and multi-temporal delta features capture seasonal canopy variations.
